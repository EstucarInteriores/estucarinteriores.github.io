About the studio

ESTUCAR INTERIORES (Estudio Carcabón) is an interior design studio based in Madrid, specialising in interior layout planning, furniture and lighting, with a technical approach and a clear working method.

Studio direction

The studio is founded and led by Carmen Martínez Riesgo-Carcabón, an interior designer with specialised training in interior design and extensive experience in managing and coordinating operational processes.

THE STUDIO

The studio was born from a way of understanding interior design as a discipline at the service of everyday life and the project, where decisions are made with criteria, order and an understanding of the full process: from analysing the space to execution.

I work from proportion, light and materiality, seeking solutions that are functional, calm and coherent with the existing architecture. It is not about imposing a style, but about making the space work better, whatever its scale or use.

CRITERIA AND METHOD

Each project is approached with a defined scope from the outset and a structured methodology that avoids improvisation:

  • Analysis of the space, real use and specific needs.
  • Clear definition of the layout and design criteria.
  • Project development in phases, adjusting the level of intervention to each case.
  • Documentation and visuals designed to support decisions and execution.

This approach makes it possible to work with confidence, both in private homes and in professional projects, and to integrate at different stages without losing coherence.

AN ORGANISED WAY OF WORKING

With more than fifteen years of previous experience in management, organisation and processes, the studio applies that foundation to interior design: clear projects, understandable documentation and direct communication.

The aim is always the same: to bring clarity, reduce friction and help the project move forward.

COLLABORATIONS

ESTUCAR INTERIORES works both directly with private clients and in collaboration with architects, contractors, developers and real estate agencies, integrating as specialist support when the project requires it.

Collaborations are always set up with defined roles, clear scope and delimited responsibility.

IN SUMMARY

Interior design with criteria and method: well-founded decisions, useful documentation and an organised way of working so the project can move forward with confidence.
