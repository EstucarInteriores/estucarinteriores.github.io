Virtual Home Staging · Madrid

Visual preparation for sale without renovation works: order, proportion and overall coherence.

Area 45 m²
Objective Make the layout easier to read and communicate the home’s potential clearly
Resolution Visual layout · Light staging · Global coherence
Context Planning Proposal Result Contact

Context

A vacant home for sale, on the market for months without traction.

The brief was to prepare the property visually for sale through virtual home staging: a 3D proposal with no physical intervention, designed to show the potential clearly and help buyers understand an open-plan layout at a glance.

In this kind of service, the value is not “decorating”, but organising: hierarchy, proportion and global coherence so the buyer perceives real scale and continuity, and can imagine day-to-day use without effort.

Planning

A clear working base to define circulation, viewpoints and spatial organisation.

The documentation anchors both the existing conditions and the logic of the proposal: usable areas, relationships between zones and key viewpoints. In home staging, this phase is what ensures the result feels credible and consistent throughout.

Proposal

Visual order, continuity, and deliberate neutrality.

The proposal is built on measured decisions: neutral furniture, a clear hierarchy between areas, and a continuous read across living, dining and a light work-support zone. The goal is for the home to feel brighter, calmer and more spacious, without imposing a style that narrows the buyer’s imagination.

Instead of “adding”, we focus on fit: correct proportions, friction-free circulation and coherence between rooms, so the home reads as one unified space.

First impression

A restrained entry, with believable scale and minimal pieces. The aim is instant clarity: a legible route and an immediate sense of order.

Whole-home coherence

The dining zone is proportioned to “lock in” with the living area: comfortable clearances, correct distances and a continuous visual rhythm.

A continuous read

The scene is designed for photography: breathing room, clear hierarchy and deliberate neutrality—so anyone can picture themselves living there.

Order without “decorating”

A restrained scene with no excess objects. The key is legibility: credible dimensions, easy circulation and a calm overall feel.

A truthful overall view

One global image that feels believable: realistic scale, coherent pieces and continuity across rooms. The goal is not to decorate, but to make the potential immediately understandable.

Result

Clearer, more coherent spaces—easy to understand at a glance, with believable scale.

The outcome stands on three ideas: a continuous whole-home read, visual order and neutrality. The property feels more balanced and easier to “read”, helping communicate its value and making the buyer’s decision simpler.
