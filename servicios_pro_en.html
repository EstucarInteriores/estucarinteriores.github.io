Services for professional teams: interior design support, documentation and visualization, integrated into projects led by third parties, with defined scope and clear deliverables.

INTERIOR DESIGN SUPPORT · CONCEPT STAGE

Support in early phases, providing interior design and spatial criteria in professional contexts, integrated within the project framework led by the responsible team.

INCLUDES

  • Review of the approach and available documentation.
  • Layout proposals and spatial relationships at a descriptive level.
  • Definition or adjustment of aesthetic and conceptual criteria.
  • Non-technical supporting graphic documentation, according to the phase.

DOES NOT INCLUDE

  • Technical execution drawings or complete construction documentation.
  • Measurements, budgets, or financial management of the works.
  • Site direction or coordination.
  • Purchasing management.

INTERIOR DESIGN GRAPHIC DOCUMENTATION

Preparation of interior design graphic documentation to support professional projects, facilitating understanding, coordination and decision-making within the scope of the base project.

INCLUDES

  • Dimensioned layout plans (according to scope).
  • Interior elevations and sections.
  • Interior construction and custom furniture details (when applicable).
  • Lighting and finishes diagrams.

DOES NOT INCLUDE

  • Architectural or engineering technical project.
  • Calculations, measurements or construction budgets.
  • Site supervision (architect of record).
  • Purchasing management or site coordination.

FULL INTERIOR DESIGN PROJECT

Development of an interior design project for residential, commercial or corporate spaces: organization, concept and aesthetic criteria integrated within the global project framework.

INCLUDES

  • Analysis of the space and functional program.
  • Interior layout proposal.
  • Definition of material, lighting and atmosphere criteria.
  • Furniture proposal at project level.
  • Interior documentation according to agreed scope.

DOES NOT INCLUDE

  • Architectural project.
  • Site supervision (architect of record).
  • Technical calculations for installations or structure.
  • Permits management.
  • Purchasing management, unless specifically contracted.

HOME STAGING · VIRTUAL

Digital staging aimed at improving visual presentation for commercialization, marketing or communication. The service focuses on generic virtual images to show spatial potential, without developing an interior design project or binding decisions.

Images are delivered exclusively as visual support, with no technical or contractual value.

INCLUDES

  • Visual analysis of the space and presentation goal.
  • Generic virtual staging proposal.
  • 3D images intended for commercial or communication use.
  • Number of images defined per assignment (minimum one image per relevant room).

DOES NOT INCLUDE

  • Interior design project.
  • Usage study or personalization of the space.
  • Redistributions or detailed spatial definition.
  • Exact dimensional validation.
  • Technical or construction drawings.
  • Execution documentation.
  • Selection of real furniture.
  • Physical intervention in the space.

ADDITIONAL SERVICES

  • 3D visualizations as a working tool and support for interior design or architectural projects based on a previously defined project.
  • Purchasing management, supplier coordination and logistics follow-up for furniture based on a previously defined project.
  • Occasional support during execution to resolve design questions and maintain project coherence, with visits agreed in advance.

The specific scope (deliverables, number of views, iterations and timelines) is always defined in the quote.

Looking for interior support? Interior design as project and site support: criteria, useful documentation and coordination of finishes/lighting, with scope, deliverables and timeline defined from the outset.
